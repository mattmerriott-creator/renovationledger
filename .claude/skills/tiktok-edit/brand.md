## Voice (on-screen text and post captions)

- Practical, direct, local, transparent about numbers. Confident, not flashy.
- Hooks lead with a real number or a real problem, never fake urgency or guru hype.
  Good: "BOUGHT FOR *$80K*. HERE'S THE PLAN." Bad: "THIS WILL MAKE YOU RICH 🤑".
- No em dashes, no double hyphens. Plain words.
- Use real details only. If a number isn't in the video or Matt's context, don't show it. Ask.
- Never: guaranteed returns, easy passive income, no risk, get rich, "I need money".
- Private money mentions stay educational ("I like showing real numbers on real deals").
- Rental copy: no protected-class language. No overpromising.
