# Content types

Four types. Each video is one type; pick from Matt's context or the footage.
Lengths are targets for the cut, CTA included. Under 30 s gets more full watches; go longer
only when the story needs it.

## 1. Initial walkthrough (just bought / under contract)

Length 25-45 s. Goal: project proof + seller trust ("houses like this are what I buy").

- **Hook (0-2 s):** the worst thing in the house or the purchase price.
  - "BOUGHT THIS ONE FOR *$80K*"
  - "THIS HOUSE SAT VACANT FOR *3 YEARS*"
  - "WOULD YOU BUY THIS HOUSE?"
- **Body:** 3-5 problem shots, each 2-4 s, each with a red callout ("ROOF LEAK", "NO HVAC").
  Then the plan in one breath: rehab budget (red), ARV or rent (green).
- **Callouts:** PURCHASE $X (yellow), problem labels (red), REHAB ~$X (red), ARV ~$X or RENT $X/MO (green).
- **End:** "Follow along for the after" said or as a caption, then CTA card.
- Bottom CTA line: "VACANT · INHERITED · TIRED RENTALS".

## 2. Rehab update (in progress)

Length 20-40 s. Goal: keep followers coming back; show competence.

- **Hook:** the biggest change since last time, or a surprise.
  - "WEEK *3*: THE KITCHEN IS GONE"
  - "FOUND THIS BEHIND THE WALL"
  - "*$6K* MISTAKE WE ALMOST MISSED"
- **Body:** what got done (quick cuts, 1-2 s each, speed up silent work footage 1.5-2x), one
  thing that went wrong or cost more, what's next.
- **Callouts:** DAY/WEEK N (yellow), costs (red), budget status ("ON BUDGET" green / "+$2K" red).
- If there's a previous update, say "part N" in the hook or caption.

## 3. How-to / education

Length 30-60 s. Goal: authority, saves and shares. Seller education and BRRRR basics
(see Chuck's content pillars).

- **Hook:** the question or the mistake.
  - "HOW I KNOW A HOUSE IS A DEAL IN *60 SECONDS*"
  - "DON'T FIX THIS BEFORE YOU SELL"
  - "THE *70%* RULE, EXPLAINED"
- **Body:** numbered steps; each step gets a yellow callout "1. ARV", "2. REPAIRS", ... at the
  moment it's named. Show the thing on screen (house, receipt, calculator) when possible.
- **Callouts:** step labels (yellow), formulas and numbers (green).
- CTA: keep the card; the bottom line can be "QUESTIONS? CALL OR TEXT".

## 4. After / reveal

Length 15-35 s. Goal: the payoff; most shareable type.

- **Hook:** the before shot or the number, at frame 0.
  - "*$40K* LATER..."
  - "SAME HOUSE. *90 DAYS*."
  - "FROM VACANT TO RENTED"
- **Body:** before/after pairs, matched angles if possible, fast (1-1.5 s each), music-driven.
  If Matt sent before footage too, alternate before → after per room.
- **Callouts:** the numbers once each: BOUGHT $X (yellow), REHAB $X (red), ARV/SOLD $X or
  RENTED $X/MO (green), and for flips the result ("+$43K") only if Matt shared it.
- Music is louder here (volume 0.22-0.3) since there's often less talking.

## Pacing rules (all types)

- Something changes every 2-4 s: a cut, a zoom, a callout, a new caption group.
- No shot of a static room longer than 3 s without speech over it.
- First 2 s: hook text + the most interesting frame. Never start on a logo, black, or Matt
  saying "hey guys".
- Cut "so", "okay", "alright" at the start of the video. Keep them mid-sentence.
- End on the CTA card while the last line or music lands. No dead air after.
